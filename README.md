# Ghostly Possession

A Halloween mod for KIF Beta. Now and then a Ghost possesses one of your team, and some wild Pokémon have one inside. Catch one and keep the Ghost or the Pokémon, or cast the Ghost out to try for both. At night the Spooky Season turns the world violet and lights up the towns.

It works on its own for now. When Kanto Reloaded is installed, it uses Kanto Reloaded's screens and settings for now until replaced, as it is originally a part of the Halloween Season for Kanto Reloaded.

## Version

- **Ghostly Possession:** 1.0.0
- **Works On:** KIF 1.2.1+

## Features

- **Team Possession:** Now and then a Ghost possesses one of your Pokémon. In battle it fights as the fusion of the two (the Ghost's head on your Pokémon's body), with the better of each stat and the Ghost's move in one of its slots, and now and then the Ghost takes a turn itself. After a few battles the Ghost leaves, sometimes lingering to fight you.
- **Casting Out:** A Cleanse Tag (now sold at Lavender Town's Poké Mart) or the Channeler in Lavender's Volunteer House casts a Ghost out, and you get a battle to catch it.
- **Haunted Wilds:** Some wild Pokémon have a Ghost inside. Catch one and choose which to keep. Weaken it and hit it with a Dark- or Ghost-type move, or use a Cleanse Tag, to tear the Ghost out and catch both. Knock it out with the Ghost still inside, and the angry Ghost may leap into your Pokémon.
- **Will-o'-Wisp Trails:** At night a wisp may appear and lead you somewhere.
- **Restless Spirits:** In KIF's Nuzlocke, a wisp may lead you to the grave of one of your fallen. Pay your respects, or call its spirit and catch it to bring it back as a Ghost.
- **Nights:**
  - **Violet Nights:** Deepest Violet, Midnight Violet, or Pitch Black, where you only see near you and near lights.
  - **Town Lights:** Windows, doors and lamps glow at night, and a few landmarks light up in their own way.
  - **Light Patches:** Every light lifts the dark round it, and you can carry a light of your own.
- **The Spooky Season:** Fog over haunted places, haunted music on routes at night, more Ghost-types at night, Poké Mart clerks in Witchy Hats, and a Witchy Hat of your own on 31 October.
- **The Looks:** A possessed Pokémon shows as the fusion on the party screen, in the PC and in its Summary, and in battle with a purple aura and a ghost mark. Now and then one wanders KIF's title screen.
- **No Fusing:** A possessed Pokémon can't be fused or unfused until the Ghost is gone.

## Supported Mods

- **Hoenn Overworld Encounters:** Possessed wild Pokémon walk the routes with their aura and flicker from place to place.
- **Alpha Encounters:** An alpha is never possessed. One that turns alpha as its battle starts stays both.

## Installing

1. Install KIF Beta.
2. Download the latest release of this mod and unzip it.
3. Put the `GhostlyPossession` folder from inside it in KIF Beta's `Mods` folder, so the path is `Mods\GhostlyPossession\mod.json`.
4. Start the game. Its settings are in KIF Settings' Pokémon section, under Ghostly Possession, Nights and Spooky Season. With Kanto Reloaded, they're in the Kanto Reloaded section.
