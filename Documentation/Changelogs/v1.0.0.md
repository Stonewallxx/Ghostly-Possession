# Ghostly Possession
## Update #1 - Spooky Season
- **Version:** v1.0.0
- **Release Date:** October 25, 2026
- **Works On:** KIF 1.2.1+
## Added
### Team Possession
- Now and then a Ghost possesses one of your Pokémon. It battles as the fusion of the two, with the better of each stat and the Ghost's move, and leaves after 5, 10 or 15 battles.
### Casting Out
- A Cleanse Tag (sold at Lavender Town's Poké Mart) or the Channeler in Lavender's Volunteer House casts a Ghost out, and you get a battle to catch it.
### Haunted Wilds
- Some wild Pokémon have a Ghost inside. Catch one and keep either, or tear the Ghost out with a Dark- or Ghost-type move or a Cleanse Tag to catch both.
### Will-o'-Wisp Trails
- At night a wisp may appear and lead you somewhere.
### Restless Spirits
- In KIF's Nuzlocke, a wisp may lead you to the grave of one of your fallen. Pay your respects, or catch its spirit to bring it back as a Ghost.
### Nights
- Violet nights in three darknesses, Town Lights, and Light Patches round every light (and round you, if you like).
### The Spooky Season
- Fog over haunted places, haunted music at night, more Ghost-types at night, Poké Mart clerks in Witchy Hats, and a Witchy Hat of your own on 31 October.
### The Looks
- A possessed Pokémon shows as the fusion on the party screen, in the PC, in its Summary and in battle.
### No Fusing
- A possessed Pokémon can't be fused or unfused until the Ghost is gone.
## Supported Mods
### Hoenn Overworld Encounters
- Possessed wild Pokémon walk the routes with their aura.
### Alpha Encounters
- An alpha is never possessed, but one can turn alpha as its battle starts and stay both.
